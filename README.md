The Fire Rises Custom Wiki

<p align="center"> <img src="assets/tfr-logo.gif" alt="The Fire Rises logo" width="180"> </p>

A browser-based lore wiki editor made to match the look of The Fire Rises Wiki. It is meant for writing and organising your own fan content.

Projects stay in the browser while you work and can be exported as standalone HTML files. Those files can be opened normally as a wiki or imported back into the editor later to continue editing.

The site is fully static and does not include or host The Fire Rises Wiki's article content.
